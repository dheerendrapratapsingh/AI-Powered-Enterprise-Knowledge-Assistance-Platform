# VIT-AP University Campus Transport: Tenali Bus Routes & Schedule

Official university daily transport schedule, bus route numbers, pickup points, and morning timings for students and day scholars commuting from Tenali to VIT-AP Campus.

## General Information
- **Destination**: VIT-AP University Campus, Inavolu, Beside AP Secretariat, Amaravati
- **Campus Arrival Time**: 8:45 AM (All buses arrive at campus by 8:45 AM for 9:00 AM classes)
- **Commute Type**: Daily morning pickup and evening return services for day scholars, faculty, and staff.
